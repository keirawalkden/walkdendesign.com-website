About me

Hi, I’m Keira Walkden, an Industrial Design student at the College for Creative Studies with a strong interest in how products function and how people interact with them. I’m particularly drawn to developing ideas into thoughtful, functional products that improve everyday experiences.

What I value

I believe good design comes from understanding the relationship between functionality, usability, and the manufacturing process. I value thoughtful details, intuitive interactions, and design decisions that serve a clear purpose.

How I work

I approach design through hands-on exploration and experimentation. I enjoy figuring out how components work together and bringing ideas to life through physical prototyping, 3D modeling, and concept sketching. Building functional prototypes helps me communicate ideas, test how they work in practice, and refine designs based on what I learn.
