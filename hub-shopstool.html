HUB

Adjustable Workshop Stool

HUB is an adjustable workshop stool that combines ergonomic seating with integrated tool storage for low-height work. Developed during a sophomore-year furniture studio, the design improves tool accessibility, accommodates different users and working styles, and creates a functional, visually appealing product.
