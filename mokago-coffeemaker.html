MOKAGO

Portable Coffee Maker

MOKAGO combines the traditional moka pot brewing experience with the convenience of a portable, battery-powered coffee maker. Developed during a sophomore-year coffee maker design studio, the design features single-serve brewing, integrated coffee storage, and USB-C charging to make fresh coffee more accessible on the go.
